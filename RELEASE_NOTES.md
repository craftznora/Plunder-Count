**English** · Download **`PlunderCount.exe`** below and run it. Nothing to install. Already using it? Press **U** in the counter: your history and settings are kept.

> **Turn on replays in Fortnite first**: *Settings → Game → Replays → Record Replays: On*. Plunder Count only reads these replay files: no game modification, no access to the game's memory. Its only network access is the update check on GitHub (can be turned off).

**What's new in 1.2.0**
- **Dial overlay**: a dial shows this match against your best match, with the road to your next milestone and your latest matches as small bars
- **Compact overlay**: the gold line now shows your progress to the next milestone
- **Dashboard**: milestone track, a star chart of your matches (treasures linked like a constellation, wins marked), and loot insights (dry spell, longest drought, best mode, peak hour)
- Built-in fonts (Sora, Geist, JetBrains Mono) and matching console colors

Windows may show *"Windows protected your PC"* (unsigned exe): *More info → Run anyway*.
